Key academic foundations, peer-reviewed baselines, and physical formulations underpinning the BharatSpectral Dual-Pillar Framework.

Foundation Model [REF-01]
SpectralGPT: Spectral Foundation Model
Hong, D., Zhang, B., Li, X., Chanussot, J., & Zhu, X. X. (2024)
IEEE TPAMI · Vol. 46, No. 8, pp. 5412–5427
Core Contribution: First 3D masked spectral-spatial generative pre-trained transformer for remote sensing with uniform random token masking.
⚡ Limitation $\rightarrow$ BharatSpectral Innovation
Rigid 3D patches ignore Red-Edge absorption density & random masking ignores atmospheric gaps $\rightarrow$ Replaced by SHT & AAM.
"SpectralGPT is the SOTA baseline, but collapses on fragmented Indian smallholder farms."
Foundation Model [REF-02]
SS-MAE: Spectral-Spatial Masked Autoencoder for HSI
Lin, Y., Gao, L., Zheng, X., & Zhang, B. (2024)
IEEE TGRS · Vol. 62, pp. 1–14
Core Contribution: Vision Transformer (ViT) Masked Autoencoder framework adapted for hyperspectral pixel/patch reconstruction.
⚡ Limitation $\rightarrow$ BharatSpectral Innovation
Standard MSE loss swallows subtle low-reflectance signals (<5% SWIR/water/carbon) $\rightarrow$ Solved by RNRL (magnitude-normalized loss).
"RNRL prevents high-reflectance land features from dominating spectral learning."
Foundation Model [REF-03]
HyperSIGMA: A Scalable Foundation Model for HSI
Wang, X., Zhang, L., & Chanussot, J. (2024)
IEEE TGRS · Vol. 62, pp. 1–16
Core Contribution: Scaled ViT architecture across multi-task spectral classification and change detection benchmarks.
⚡ Limitation $\rightarrow$ BharatSpectral Innovation
Assumes fixed single-sensor band counts; cannot ingest AVIRIS-NG (425), EMIT (285), and HysIS (220) into one latent space $\rightarrow$ Replaced by SSPE.
"Multi-sensor ingestion requires joint GSD, bandwidth, and entropy encoding."
Scale Encoding [REF-04]
Scale-MAE: High-Resolution MAE for Downstream Tasks
Reed, C. J., Metzger, R., Srinivas, A., Darrell, T., & Keutzer, K. (2023)
IEEE/CVF ICCV · pp. 14288–14299
Core Contribution: Introduced Ground Sample Distance (GSD) positional conditioning into ViTs for multi-resolution aerial imagery.
⚡ Extension $\rightarrow$ BharatSpectral Innovation
Scale-MAE only encodes 2D spatial GSD for RGB $\rightarrow$ Extended to 3D continuous spectral bandwidth and sub-pixel mixture entropy in SSPE.
"Extended spatial GSD encoding to 3D hyperspectral physics."
CNN Baseline [REF-05]
HybridSN: 3D-2D CNN Feature Hierarchy for HSI
Roy, S. K., Krishna, G., Dubey, S. R., & Chaudhuri, B. B. (2020)
IEEE GRSL · Vol. 17, No. 8, pp. 1352–1356
Core Contribution: Combined joint 3D spatial-spectral convolutions with 2D spatial kernels to extract hierarchical features.
⚡ Benchmark Gap $\rightarrow$ BharatSpectral Evidence
Overfits to rectilinear mono-crop spatial textures. Experiences a 37.3% accuracy collapse on Indian smallholder plots $\rightarrow$ Justifies Transformer attention.
"Canonical CNN baseline proving spatial overfitting on fragmented farms."
CNN Baseline [REF-06]
3D Deep Learning Approach for RS Image Classification
Ben Hamida, A., Benoit, A., Lambert, P., & Ben Amar, C. (2018)
IEEE TGRS · Vol. 56, No. 8, pp. 4429–4442
Core Contribution: Established pure 3D convolutional architectures for direct spectral-spatial classification without manual PCA/MNF reduction.
⚡ Benchmark Baseline $\rightarrow$ Model Zoo
Serves as the classical 3D convolution benchmark in our comparative Model Zoo across Indian Pines vs. AVIRIS-NG India target scenes.
"Direct spectral-spatial baseline for evaluating zero-shot cross-scene transfer."
Radiative Transfer [REF-07]
PROSPECT + SAIL: Leaf & Canopy Radiative Transfer
Jacquemoud, S. & Baret, F. (1990) / Verhoef, W. (1984)
Remote Sensing of Environment · Vol. 34(2) & 16(2)
Core Contribution: Physics equations linking leaf biochemicals ($C_{ab}, C_w, C_m$) and LAI to directional canopy spectral reflectance.
⚡ Physics Grounding $\rightarrow$ Tokenization & Loss
Direct basis for focusing SHT tokens on Chlorophyll (680nm), Water (970/1450nm), and SWIR Carbon/Cellulose (2100-2200nm).
"Theoretical foundation for diagnostic absorption bands across Indian crops."
Spectral Unmixing [REF-08]
Hyperspectral Unmixing Overview: Geometrical & Statistical
Bioucas-Dias, J. M., Plaza, A., Dobigeon, N., et al. (2012)
IEEE JSTARS · Vol. 5, No. 2, pp. 354–379
Core Contribution: Comprehensive survey of FCLS, SAM, LSU, and non-linear scattering algorithms powering tools in ENVI and QGIS.
⚡ Physical GIS Baseline $\rightarrow$ Deep Learning Fusion
Linear unmixing fails in multi-tier intercropped canopies $\rightarrow$ BharatSpectral integrates unmixing priors into ECSA & FASU.
"Defines our deterministic physical baseline (RMSE_A = 0.198) that AI surpasses."
PEFT / LoRA [REF-09]
LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models
Hu, E. J., Shen, Y., Wallis, P., Allen-Zhu, Z., Li, Y., et al. (2022)
ICLR · International Conference on Learning Representations
Core Contribution: Freezes pre-trained foundation weights and injects trainable low-rank rank decomposition matrices ($W = W_0 + B \cdot A$).
⚡ Agricultural Extension $\rightarrow$ Phenology Conditioning
Static LoRA cannot handle multi-season phenological shifts $\rightarrow$ Modulated by Kharif/Rabi/Zaid season embeddings in Ph-LoRA.
"Enables dynamic agricultural parameter adaptation without catastrophic forgetting."
Self-Supervised ViT [REF-10]
Masked Autoencoders Are Scalable Vision Learners
He, K., Chen, X., Xie, S., Li, Y., Dollár, P., & Girshick, R. (2022)
IEEE/CVF CVPR · pp. 16000–16009
Core Contribution: Asymmetric encoder-decoder architecture pre-training large ViTs by masking high fractions (75%) of spatial patches.
⚡ Architectural Heritage $\rightarrow$ BharatSpectral-MAE
Foundation of self-supervised learning, transformed with physics-informed 3D tokenization and structured atmospheric masking.
"The core self-supervised paradigm adapted to hyperspectral spectroscopy."
